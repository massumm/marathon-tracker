Delete Account

RunMate  ·  XOR Geek

This action is permanent. Deleting your account removes all your personal data, run history, and stats from RunMate. This cannot be undone.

How to Delete Your Account

You can delete your account in two ways:

1

From the App (Instant)

Open RunMate → tap your profile → Settings → Delete Account. Your account and all data will be permanently removed immediately.

2

By Email Request

Send an email to support@xorgeek.com from your registered email address with the subject "Delete My Account". We will process your request within 7 days.

What Data Is Deleted vs. Kept

Data deleted immediately upon account removal:

Account (email, name, photo)Deleted
Run history & statisticsDeleted
Run photosDeleted
Friends listDeleted
Live location dataDeleted
Push notification tokenDeleted

Data retained after deletion:

Anonymised aggregate event statsKept (anonymised)

No personally identifiable information is retained after account deletion.
